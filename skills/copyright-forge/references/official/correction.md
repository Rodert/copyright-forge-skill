# Correction Guidance

Correction notices are inputs to analyze, not proof that an earlier material is
wrong in every respect. Preserve the notice, map each item to the relevant
fact, evidence, source, or document artifact, and create a revised draft only
where the evidence supports it. Re-run all quality gates after revision.

| Rule ID | Classification | Requirement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CFS-CORRECTION-001 | best_practice | Explain the affected artifact, likely cause, proposed change, and remaining human confirmation. |
| CFS-CORRECTION-002 | best_practice | Never promise that a correction will be accepted or replace required legal evidence. |
