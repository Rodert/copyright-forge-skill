# Submission Guidance

The official application form and current submission workflow belong to the
official registration channel. Copyright Forge produces a filling guide and
preflight report; it does not recreate, alter, or submit official forms.

| Rule ID | Classification | Requirement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CN-SCR-FORM-001 | official_service_guidance | Verify the current form and submission instructions through `CN-SCR-SERVICE-PORTAL` before submission. |
| CFS-SUBMISSION-001 | best_practice | Mark every preflight finding as blocker, high, medium, or information and distinguish it from legal advice. |
